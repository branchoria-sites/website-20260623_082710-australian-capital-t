## Introduction

The flattened reed circle at Horseshoe Lagoon is the single most important piece of physical evidence in the 1966 [Tully saucer nest]({{ 'tully-nest/' | relative_url }}) case. Unlike most UFO reports, which depend entirely on witness testimony, this incident left a visible trace that could be photographed, measured and examined. For believers, the circle suggested that something unusual had physically interacted with the swamp. For sceptics, it became a test case in how easily a natural feature can be transformed into a mystery once public attention arrives. More than half a century later, the debate is less about the object George Pedley reported seeing and more about what the reed formation itself can and cannot prove. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/queenslandstatearchives/50704411782" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: flickr.com">[Flickr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">flickr.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unidentifiable Flying Objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The reeds are all flattened in a clockwise direction and surrounded by healthy green reeds. ## Did the reed circle prove anything?

The strongest argument in favour of the Tully case has always been simple: there really was a circular formation in the reeds. Contemporary photographs show a distinct flattened area in Horseshoe Lagoon, and police officers, journalists and local residents all confirmed that a visible feature existed. This was not a case in which investigators arrived to find nothing at all. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/queenslandstatearchives/50704411782" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: flickr.com">[Flickr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">flickr.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unidentifiable Flying Objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The reeds are all flattened in a clockwise direction and surrounded by healthy green reeds. However, the existence of a trace is not the same as proof of its cause. The reed circle demonstrated that something had disturbed the vegetation. It did not demonstrate that a spacecraft had landed there. The dispute has therefore centred on whether the pattern was so unusual that ordinary explanations could be ruled out, or whether the circle merely looked extraordinary because it was viewed through the lens of an already sensational sighting report. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://archive.org/stream/Australian_Flying_Saucer_Review_1966_11_no_9_UFOIC/Australian_Flying_Saucer_Review_1966_11_no_9_UFOIC_djvu.txt" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: archive.org">[Internet Archive]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">archive.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Australian Flying Saucer Review 1966 11 no 9 UFOIC djvu.txt</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">bush and weather could not accept official explanations. the reeds in the circle were killed and not all the reeds in...</span></span></span>

## What the photographed nest reportedly looked like

Early descriptions portrayed the main formation as a roughly circular patch about 30 feet (around nine metres) across. Reeds were reported to have been pressed down in a clockwise pattern, creating what observers called a "nest" appearance. Contemporary newspaper accounts noted that the flattened area stood out from the surrounding reeds and appeared relatively well defined. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/queenslandstatearchives/50704411782" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: flickr.com">[Flickr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">flickr.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unidentifiable Flying Objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The reeds are all flattened in a clockwise direction and surrounded by healthy green reeds. ### The problem of varying descriptions

One difficulty for later researchers is that measurements and descriptions were not always consistent. Different reports gave slightly different dimensions, while later retellings sometimes described a more perfect circle than appears in surviving photographs. Additional formations reportedly found nearby also varied in size and orientation, with some accounts describing anti-clockwise flattening rather than clockwise flattening. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/queenslandstatearchives/50704411782" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: flickr.com">[Flickr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">flickr.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unidentifiable Flying Objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The reeds are all flattened in a clockwise direction and surrounded by healthy green reeds. These variations do not necessarily mean the original reports were wrong, but they make it harder to treat the nests as a single, precisely documented phenomenon.

## Why the trace impressed police and investigators

The reed circle attracted attention because it seemed to resist several obvious explanations.

Police reportedly noted that if machinery or people had created the formation from the ground, some approach route or disturbance through the surrounding vegetation might be expected. Contemporary reports stated that such tracks were not readily apparent. This did not prove an aerial origin, but it helped convince investigators that the site deserved examination. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/queenslandstatearchives/50704411782" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: flickr.com">[Flickr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">flickr.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unidentifiable Flying Objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The reeds are all flattened in a clockwise direction and surrounded by healthy green reeds. The formation also appeared soon after Pedley claimed to have seen a saucer-shaped object rise from the lagoon. The proximity between the reported sighting and the physical trace gave the story a coherence lacking in many UFO cases. Instead of asking people to accept a witness account alone, supporters could point to something tangible in the swamp. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.flickr.com/photos/queenslandstatearchives/50704411782" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: flickr.com">[Flickr]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">flickr.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Unidentifiable Flying Objects</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The reeds are all flattened in a clockwise direction and surrounded by healthy green reeds. Samples were collected and examined. Radiation testing reportedly failed to reveal anything unusual, and botanical examination did not identify clear signs of burning, exotic residues or other unmistakable evidence of advanced technology. ## How visitors, water and variable measurements weakened the evidence

The physical trace was strongest immediately after discovery and became weaker as time passed.

One major problem was contamination. The site rapidly became a tourist attraction. Hundreds of curious visitors reportedly travelled to Horseshoe Lagoon, walking around and sometimes through the area. Once a trace site is heavily visited, it becomes increasingly difficult to distinguish original features from later disturbance. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-11-24/sugar-cane-farm-ufo-mystery-expanse-podcast-series-uncropped/104559256" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc.net.au">[ABC News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc.net.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">sugar cane farm ufo mystery expanse podcast series uncropped</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC NewsTully&#x27;s cane farm crop circles and an enduring 58yo UFO...23 Nov 2024 — The Tully saucer nest in Horeshoe Lagoon in 1966. The lagoon environment created further complications. The formation existed in floating reeds and swamp vegetation, an environment that naturally changes due to water movement, weather and plant growth. Investigators were not studying a fixed impression in dry soil but a dynamic wetland feature capable of changing shape over time. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://janmeecham.wordpress.com/2020/05/30/ufos-and-crop-circles-fact-or-fiction/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: janmeecham.wordpress.com">[Roger (Jan) Meecham]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">janmeecham.wordpress.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Roger (Jan) Meecham UFOs and Crop Circles, Fact or Fiction &#124; Roger (Jan) Meecham</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">George rushed to the farmhouse and told the owner of the farm, Albert Pennisi, who returned to the...Read more...</span></span></span>
