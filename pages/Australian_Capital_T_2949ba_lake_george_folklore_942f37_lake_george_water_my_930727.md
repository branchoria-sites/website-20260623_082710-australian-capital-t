## Introduction

[Lake George]({{ 'lake-george/' | relative_url }})’s reputation as a UFO hotspot owes less to documented aerial mysteries than to a much older mystery: the lake itself. Sitting just outside the [Australian Capital Territory]({{ 'australian-capital-territory/' | relative_url }}) region on the road between Canberra and Sydney, Lake George can appear as a vast inland sea one decade and an almost empty plain the next. For generations, those dramatic changes encouraged stories about hidden forces operating beneath the landscape. Long before UFO folklore became attached to the area, locals were already debating subterranean rivers, secret underground channels and unexplained disappearances of water. Those ideas helped create the atmosphere in which later reports of strange lights and unusual objects felt plausible to many observers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://hercanberra.com.au/city/the-mysteries-of-lake-george-fact-or-folklore/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hercanberra.com.au">[HerCanberra]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hercanberra.com.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Her Canberra The mysteries of Lake George: fact or folklore?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The mysteries of Lake George: fact or folklore?February 27, 2019 — 28 Feb 2019 — The lake&#x27;s dramatic water fluctuations have g...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 27, 2019</span></span></span>

## Why the lake's water levels create mystery

Lake George is an endorheic basin, meaning water does not flow out through a normal river system. Its level depends largely on rainfall, inflow from local catchments and evaporation. Because the lake is broad, shallow and highly sensitive to climatic changes, it can fill, shrink or disappear in ways that seem dramatic to casual observers.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://yumi-sabe.aiatsis.gov.au/sites/default/files/outputs/2024-05/08120099.2020.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: yumi-sabe.aiatsis.gov.au">[aiatsis.gov.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">yumi-sabe.aiatsis.gov.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ly to changes in dec- adal climate fluctuations—primarily rainfall and evapor-.Read more...</span></span></span>

What makes the effect especially striking is timescale. Travellers who pass the lake every few years may encounter completely different landscapes. A person who remembers a large body of water can return to find grazing land. Someone who first saw a dry plain may later see a substantial lake stretching towards the horizon. Historical records show these fluctuations have occurred repeatedly over at least two centuries.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://doi.pangaea.de/10.1594/PANGAEA.922463" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: doi.pangaea.de">[pangaea.de]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">doi.pangaea.de</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Two centuries of water level records at Lake George, NSW</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">June 21, 2020 — 8 Sept 2020 — Here, we have compiled all available historic water level data for Lake George, referenced them to common d...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: June 21, 2020</span></span></span>

The lake can also appear to change rapidly because strong winds push shallow water across the basin. Observers have reported noticeable short-term shifts in water position and depth, adding to the impression that something unusual is happening beneath the surface.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.australiangeographic.com.au/blogs/tim-the-yowie-man/2020/11/what-causes-lakes-georges-water-levels-to-fluctuate-so-wildly/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: australiangeographic.com.au">[Australian Geographic]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">australiangeographic.com.au</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Australian GeographicWhat causes Lake&#x27;s George&#x27;s water levels to fluctuate so...November 25, 2020 — 25 Nov 2020 — While the myth that La...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 25, 2020</span></span></span>

## From subterranean rivers to strange lights

The best-known Lake George myths revolve around hidden water connections. For many years, stories circulated that the lake was linked through underground tunnels to distant lakes elsewhere in Australia or even overseas. Some versions claimed that when Lake George emptied, another lake in China, Siberia or across the Tasman somehow filled in response. Others suggested giant subterranean canals or sinkholes beneath the lakebed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://hercanberra.com.au/city/the-mysteries-of-lake-george-fact-or-folklore/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hercanberra.com.au">[com.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hercanberra.com.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Her Canberra The mysteries of Lake George: fact or folklore?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The mysteries of Lake George: fact or folklore?February 27, 2019 — 28 Feb 2019 — The lake&#x27;s dramatic water fluctuations have g...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 27, 2019</span></span></span>

Yet the underground-river stories served an important cultural role. They encouraged people to view the lake as a place where ordinary rules might not apply. Once a landscape acquires that reputation, additional legends tend to attach themselves to it. Over time, Lake George accumulated tales involving bunyips, apparitions, unexplained disappearances and occasional reports of unusual lights. Local mystery writers and folklore collectors have frequently grouped UFO stories alongside these other legends rather than treating them as separate, evidence-based cases.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://hercanberra.com.au/city/the-mysteries-of-lake-george-fact-or-folklore/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hercanberra.com.au">[com.au]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hercanberra.com.au</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Her Canberra The mysteries of Lake George: fact or folklore?</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The mysteries of Lake George: fact or folklore?February 27, 2019 — 28 Feb 2019 — The lake&#x27;s dramatic water fluctuations have g...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 27, 2019</span></span></span>

This does not mean every report was invented. It means the cultural environment already encouraged mysterious interpretations. A distant light, unusual reflection or atmospheric effect was more likely to be discussed as part of an existing tradition of unexplained phenomena.
